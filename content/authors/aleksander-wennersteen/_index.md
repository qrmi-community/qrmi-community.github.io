Aleksander Wennersteen is the Technical Lead for Quantum-Centric HPC at Pasqal,
where he focuses on integrating quantum processors with classical HPC systems
and enabling portable hybrid quantum-classical workflows. His work spans
resource management, runtime systems, scheduling, observability, and execution
environments for cloud and on-premises quantum-HPC platforms. He is part of the
founding team of the Quantum Resource Management Interface (QRMI) and one of the
core maintainers of QRMI and its integration with HPC resource managers.
Previously at Pasqal, he worked as a quantum software engineer and technical
lead on quantum libraries, cloud platform software, and numerical simulation of
quantum systems. He holds a master’s degree in Mathematical Physics from the
University of Edinburgh. Before joining Pasqal, he worked in high-performance
computing and artificial intelligence.
