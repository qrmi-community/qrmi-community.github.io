Munetaka Ohtani is a Senior Software Engineer at IBM Quantum, specializing in
distributed systems such as Hadoop, enterprise software, cloud computing, and
HPC (high-performance computing) software development. Since 2021, he has
focused on quantum-computing infrastructure software and quantum-centric
supercomputing. His work includes software development, including simulators,
for integrating IBM Quantum System Two, deployed at national laboratory HPC data
centers, with HPC infrastructure; R&D on integrating quantum computers with HPC
schedulers; and building observability infrastructure for quantum systems,
including the definition of key metrics. Since 2025, he has been organizing the
open-source QRMI (Quantum Resource Management Interface) project, which he
launched to provide a unified interface for integrating heterogeneous QPU
backends into HPC schedulers such as Slurm and PBS. He currently serves as the
QRMI maintainer. He has also delivered a related tutorial at SCA/HPC Asia 2026,
introducing QRMI-based quantum-HPC integration to quantum vendors and HPC
communities.
