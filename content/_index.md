The Quantum Resource Management Interface (QRMI) exposes quantum processors as
schedulable resources alongside CPUs and GPUs, independent of vendor or
scheduler. It is an open-source project developed by national laboratories,
institutions and quantum vendors.

This site is run by the QRMI community and collects tutorials, papers and
integration projects built around QRMI, many of them maintained outside the
core project. For QRMI itself, see the official documentation at
[qiskit-community.github.io/qrmi](https://qiskit-community.github.io/qrmi/).

QRMI integrates with Slurm, PBS, IBM LSF, Open Cluster Scheduler and Flux,
with Kubernetes support under development. See the [ecosystem]({{< relref "ecosystem" >}}) for all projects.
